I aim to develop intelligent systems that learn models of complex environments from data and interaction, with little reliance on human-labeled data. I am interested in how self-supervised learning, self-play and search can turn increasing computation and experience into better decisions and ultimately superhuman performance. Understanding these systems is as important to me as building them. I want to explain why their strategies succeed, determine under what conditions they are optimal, and investigate what their learned models reveal about the environments they represent. By developing and analyzing intelligent systems, I aim to advance our fundamental understanding of intelligence and of complex systems such as human society and markets.
